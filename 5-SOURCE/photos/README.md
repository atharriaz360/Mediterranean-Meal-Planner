# Real food photos (optional)

Drop JPEGs here. `python3 build.py --sync` embeds them into the app, demo and online build. Any dish without a file keeps its illustration.

**Format:** `.jpg`, 4:3, **800×600**, quality ~70 (under 90 KB each, so the app stays light).
**Rights:** use only photos you own, made yourself, or licensed for commercial resale inside a product (check the licence — Unsplash/Pexels licences do not allow repackaging photos in a product you sell). AI-generated photos you create yourself are fine.
