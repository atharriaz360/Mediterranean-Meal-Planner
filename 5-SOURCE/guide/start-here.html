Thank you for your purchase! Mediterranean Meal Planner is your private Mediterranean meal planner. It takes less than a minute to set up. There’s no account and nothing to install.

💻 On your computer

  1. Unzip the download if needed, then move Mediterranean-Meal-Planner.html somewhere it will stay, such as your Documents folder.
  2. Double-click it. It opens in your web browser.
  3. Bookmark the page so it’s one click away.

📱 On your phone or tablet

  1. Open your online link (below) in Safari (iPhone/iPad) or Chrome (Android).
  2. iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
    Android: tap ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Mediterranean Meal Planner now opens like an app and works offline.

✨ Your first five minutes

  1. Mediterranean Meal Planner opens with a sample week. Click around: Week Plan, Recipes, Shopping.
  2. Open a recipe and tap Plan it. Then tap Build shopping list and watch the ingredients appear, sorted by aisle.
  3. When you’re ready, go to Settings → Clear samples, and make it yours.

🛟 The one rule: your data lives in your browser

Mediterranean Meal Planner saves automatically, privately, on this device only. So: use the same browser each time, don’t open the file from inside a zip or email preview, and tap “Back up now” in Settings once a week. Mediterranean Meal Planner reminds you. A backup is a small file you can restore on any device.

Good to know

Moving to a new device

On the old device: Settings → Back up now. On the new one: open Mediterranean Meal Planner → Settings → Restore backup and pick that file.

Printing

Week Plan → Print gives a landscape week for the fridge. Shopping → Print gives a tick-off list. Any recipe → Print gives a recipe card.

Make it yours

Settings has dark mode, 5 accent colours, week start day, water goal, and an option to hide calories entirely.

Troubleshooting

“My planner is empty!”You may have opened it in a different browser, or moved the file to a new folder. Open it the way you did before, or restore your latest backup. A yellow “can’t save” warning appears.You’re in a preview window (Files app, email, zip) or private browsing. Open the file in Safari/Chrome/Edge/Firefox, or use your online link. “Storage full.”Remove a few recipe photos, then back up. Photos are shrunk automatically, so this is rare. Check everything works: Settings → Help → Run check.

Works with
